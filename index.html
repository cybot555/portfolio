Final Project HCI Computer Vision

CS152 Final Version

Still There: A Computer Vision Attendance and Engagement Verification System

Still There is a web-based classroom attendance and engagement verification system that uses computer vision and machine learning to help instructors monitor student presence and attentiveness in real time. It combines facial recognition for automated attendance with behavioral analysis of gaze direction, head pose, eye openness, and posture-related cues, then presents the results through live session monitoring, evidence snapshots, and analytics dashboards.

Award Reflection

A short reflection on the CCIS Innovision 2026 recognition.

Winning Best Machine Learning Model reminded me that strong results come from disciplined teamwork as much as technical skill. As one of the developers behind Still There, I learned how important it is to trust each member’s strengths, communicate clearly, and keep moving even when the project feels too ambitious. The work was demanding because the concept required both careful design and a reliable technical foundation, but the pressure also pushed us to become more focused and resourceful. Presenting the project and seeing it recognized validated the long hours we invested. More than the award itself, the experience taught me how persistence, collaboration, and belief in the project can turn a difficult idea into meaningful output.
